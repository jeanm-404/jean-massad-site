# Jean Massad

Jean Massad is a design lead at Surge AI and runs Konpo, a design studio.

Design from napkin sketch through final_final release.

In the design trenches ever since I got Photoshop off a sketchy torrent site. Think of me as an .md file but, like, human.

I'm Jean Massad, a design lead at [Surge AI](https://www.surgehq.ai) who also runs [Konpo](https://www.konpo.studio), a nimble design studio. I work through the design spectrum across brand, product and systems for startups, F500, gov and everything in between.

My work has won over Awwwards and the Webbys, survived Product Hunt, been torn apart on Hacker News, shown up behind Tim Cook in a keynote, been loved by Terry Crews, made the cover of Forbes and smiled from the top of the App Store.
